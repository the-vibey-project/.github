# Code of Conduct

This is the organisation-wide default for
[the-vibey-project](https://github.com/the-vibey-project). A repository that
ships its own overrides it.

## The standard

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to build something and
has less context than you do.

Concretely, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions:

- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This drops the lease on a retry" is a
  review. "You clearly didn't think about this" is not.
- **Say what you mean plainly.** Directness is welcome; contempt is not. A blunt
  technical disagreement is fine. Sarcasm aimed at someone is not.
- **Show your evidence.** A claim about behaviour should come with the command,
  the output, or the line. This is a project where the rules are enforced by
  tests rather than by argument, and the same standard applies to conversation.
- **Let people be wrong without being humiliated.** Most bad patches are missing
  context, not judgement.

Not acceptable, anywhere in these repositories: harassment or personal attacks;
sexualised language or imagery; demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
background, or level of experience; publishing anyone's private information;
sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every space these projects occupy — repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any public space where you are representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report anything that concerns you to **adam@matthewsteinberger.com**. Reports are
read by the maintainer and are not shared further without your agreement.

Expect an acknowledgement within 3 working days. Responses are proportionate: a
private word, a request to edit or withdraw a comment, a temporary block, or a
permanent one. Deliberate harassment goes straight to the last of those.

This project is maintained by one person. That means enforcement is honest but
not instantaneous, and it means the maintainer is held to this document too — if
the problem is the maintainer's conduct, say so plainly in the same channel.
